## 🧐 About <a name = "about"></a>
The game follows the story of a young student who, through a mistake in their research, triggers an anachronism, transporting them to an unknown and ancient world. They must adapt to this new world where everything is unfamiliar and escape the guardians of each world they traverse. By surviving and collecting bonuses along the way, they progress through different worlds that progressively resemble the present from which they came, with each world representing a different level.

## 🎈 Features <a name = "Features"></a>

- Explore ancient Egyptian landscapes with an African twist.
- Escape enemies and traps to progress through levels.
- Collect bonuses and items to increase your score and chances of survival.
- Engaging storyline that unfolds as you progress through the game.
- Beautifully hand-drawn graphics and animations.
- Original soundtrack that immerses you in the ancient Egyptian atmosphe

## 🏁 Getting Started : How to Play <a name = "getting_started"></a>

   - Use the arrow keys to move left and right.
   - Press the spacebar or the up arrow key to jump.
   - Avoid enemies and obstacles to stay alive.
   - Collect bonuses and items to increase your score.
   - Reach the end of each level to progress to the next.

## 🚀 Installation <a name = "Installation"></a>
